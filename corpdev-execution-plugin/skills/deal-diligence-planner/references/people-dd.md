# Module — People / HR Diligence

DRI: **People Ops** (with CEO for key-employee retention). In scope when employees transfer. **Goes
deepest for talent acqui-hires**, where the team *is* the thesis.

## Request list (trim to the deal)

**Roster & comp** *(P0 for acqui-hires)*
- Full employee list: name, title, role, tenure, YoE, location, comp (base/bonus/equity)
- Comp/leveling mapping to Toast bands; identify over/under-market and re-leveling needs
- Contractor list (with classification — coordinate with legal-dd)

**Key-person & retention** *(P0 when team is the thesis)*
- Identify key employees (founders, leads, critical ICs); flight risk read
- Retention-grant plan sizing (with CEO); proposed treatment of unvested equity
- Founder/exec intentions post-close (vesting, roles, earnout participation)

**Classification & compliance**
- Employee vs. contractor exposure by geo; misclassification liability
- International employees/contractors → entity / employer-of-record needs, **immigration** (long-lead)
- Benefits, PTO/accrual, 401(k), any pension/deferred-comp obligations

**Interviews**
- All-employee interviews (default). For teams **>10 engineers, phase in groups of ~5**; request more
  recommendations if the initial set is insufficient. Optional/abbreviated for small deals.

## Priorities
- **P0:** key-person retention, classification exposure, immigration lead times
- **P1:** comp/leveling gaps, benefits transition complexity
- **P2:** full org mapping, culture read

## Out / light when
Light only if no employees transfer (rare). Otherwise always in; depth set by team size and how
central the team is to the thesis.

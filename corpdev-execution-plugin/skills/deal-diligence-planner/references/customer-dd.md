# Module — Customer Diligence

DRI: **Corp Dev + Product**. In scope when there are paying customers. Validates the retention and
concentration assertions and de-risks the revenue base.

## Request list (trim to the deal)

**Retention & cohorts** *(P0 when retention is the thesis)*
- Logo and dollar retention; GRR/NRR (reconcile to financial-dd)
- Cohort curves; churn reasons; downgrade/expansion drivers

**Concentration & quality** *(P0 when "no concentration" is an assertion)*
- Revenue concentration (top 10 customers as % of ARR); contract terms/lengths
- Renewal calendar near close; at-risk accounts
- Customer contracts' **change-of-control** posture (feeds closing consents)

**Voice of customer**
- **Blind reference calls** — masked so as not to tip the deal; structured questions on value,
  stickiness, alternatives, expansion intent
- Direct customer interviews where the target permits (later-stage, NDA-covered)
- Proof-of-concept / CAB validation (optional)

**Pipeline & GTM**
- Win/loss; sales-cycle length; competitive displacement
- Support/CSAT signals; NPS if available

## Priorities
- **P0:** retention validating the thesis, concentration risk, near-term renewal risk
- **P1:** blind references, win/loss
- **P2:** NPS, CAB

## Method note
Keep customer diligence **masked** until late and NDA-covered — premature customer contact risks
leaks and tips the deal. Blind references first; named interviews only with the target's sign-off.

## Out / light when
Out for pre-revenue acqui-hires (no customers). Real for tuck-ins; deep cohort/concentration work for
platform deals.

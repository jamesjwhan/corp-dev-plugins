# Module — Tax & Accounting Diligence

DRI: **Tax / Accounting**. Always in scope. Surfaces structure implications and material exposures
that affect price, reps/indemnities, and the post-close accounting.

## Request list (trim to the deal)

**Structure & deal-tax**
- Stock vs. asset vs. merger; availability/benefit of 338(h)(10) / 336(e) elections
- Transaction-cost treatment: under **ASC 805**, a business combination **expenses** transaction
  costs as incurred (US GAAP); tax treatment may diverge (IRC §263 capitalization), creating a
  deferred tax asset. Asset acquisitions capitalize — confirm which regime applies.
- Transfer taxes; sales/use tax nexus and exposure; state apportionment

**Material tax exposures** *(P0 when cross-border or complex)*
- Open audits, notices, uncertain tax positions; NOLs and §382 limitations
- Payroll-tax compliance; multi-state/international registrations
- R&D credits; deferred tax assets/liabilities; valuation allowances

**Accounting quality** (coordinate with financial-dd)
- Revenue-recognition policy and consistency; deferred revenue
- Significant estimates/accruals; related-party transactions
- Audit history / quality of records

**Post-close accounting setup** (feeds Stage 6 close-out)
- PPA/intangibles valuation planning (long-lead); opening balance sheet; goodwill

## Priorities
- **P0:** material tax liabilities, structure that affects after-tax value, restatement/rev-rec risk
- **P1:** NOL/credit availability, nexus exposure
- **P2:** housekeeping, minor positions

## Out / light when
Always at least light (structure + ASC 805 always matter). Deep for cross-border, multi-entity, or
revenue-heavy deals.

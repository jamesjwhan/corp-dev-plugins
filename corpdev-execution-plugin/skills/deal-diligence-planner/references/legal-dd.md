# Module — Legal Diligence

DRI: **Legal**. Always in scope; depth scales with deal complexity. Output feeds the reps/warranties/
indemnities in the definitive agreement and surfaces deal-breakers early.

## Request list (trim to the deal)

**Corporate / structure**
- Cap table, charter, bylaws, board/stockholder consents, prior financing docs (SAFEs, notes, preferred terms)
- Stockholder list + signatures/drag-along capacity (feeds closing stockholder package)
- Subsidiaries, foreign entities, good-standing

**IP** *(P0 when "clean IP" is an assertion)*
- IP assignment agreements for all employees/contractors (gaps = key-person/ownership risk)
- Patents, trademarks, domains; chain of title
- Inbound/outbound licenses; **open-source usage + obligations** (coordinate with technical-dd)
- Any IP litigation, claims, or encumbrances

**Contracts**
- Material customer contracts / MSAs — **change-of-control / assignment clauses** (feeds closing consents)
- Key vendor/partner agreements; leases; debt instruments
- Exclusivity, MFN, most-favored terms, unusual liabilities

**Employment / people-legal**
- Employment agreements, offer letters, non-competes/non-solicits (enforceability by geo)
- Contractor vs. employee classification exposure (coordinate with people-dd)
- Any employment claims/disputes

**Litigation & regulatory**
- Pending/threatened litigation; consent decrees; regulatory actions
- Privacy/security posture: data-processing agreements, GDPR/CCPA, breach history
- Licenses/permits and transferability

## Priorities
- **P0:** IP ownership/contamination, change-of-control consents, material litigation, classification exposure
- **P1:** contract terms affecting value, privacy posture, license transferability
- **P2:** corporate housekeeping, good-standing

## Out / light when
Light for a clean pre-revenue acqui-hire (focus on IP assignments + employment); full for platform
deals with customers, debt, and regulatory surface.

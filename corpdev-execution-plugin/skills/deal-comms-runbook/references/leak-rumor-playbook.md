# Reference — Leak / Rumor Playbook

Active through the **entire** window — from term sheet through announcement. Leak risk peaks during a
deferred sign-to-close (more people under the tent, more time). Owner: **Comms + Corp Dev**.

## Standing posture (pre-announcement)
- **Default response to any inquiry: neither confirm nor deny.** Use the approved holding statement.
- Keep the tent small; enforce the acknowledgment form; social-media blackout request with the target.
- Monitor: press, social, industry channels, Glassdoor/Blind, the target's social footprint.

## Holding statements (prepare in advance, approved by Legal/Comms)
- **Press inquiry:** a brief "we don't comment on rumors or speculation" line.
- **Employee inquiry (internal leak):** acknowledge awareness of speculation, reiterate confidentiality,
  point to when/how official info will come.
- **Customer inquiry:** reassurance of continuity without confirming.

## Escalation tree
1. Inquiry/leak detected → log it (source, reach, content) in the comms tracker.
2. Notify Comms lead + Deal Lead immediately.
3. Assess reach/credibility → decide: hold, accelerate announcement, or issue holding statement.
4. Material leak at a public company → loop **Legal/IR** (Reg FD / disclosure implications) fast.
5. Document the response and outcome.

## Accelerate-announcement trigger
If a credible leak threatens to break the story, the plan should pre-authorize a path to **pull the
announcement forward** — pre-drafted assets and a compressed run-of-show make this possible. This is
why assets are drafted early even when announcement is weeks out.

## Output for the plan
A short leak/rumor section: standing posture, the three holding statements (drafted), the escalation
tree, the monitoring list, and the accelerate trigger — all human-approved, none auto-issued.

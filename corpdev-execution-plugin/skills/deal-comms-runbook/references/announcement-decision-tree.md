# Reference — Announcement Decision Tree

Choose the announcement posture from the deal's characteristics. Owner: **Comms + Deal Sponsor**
(materiality/8-K → Legal/IR).

## Step 1 — Is external announcement warranted at all?

| Signal | Lean |
|---|---|
| Strategically significant; talent/product the market cares about; competitive narrative to set | **Proactive** |
| Small acqui-hire, no product story, low external interest | **Low-key / reactive-only** |
| Acquirer is public and deal is **material** | Announcement is effectively required (8-K); make it deliberate |

## Step 2 — Pick the posture

- **Proactive PR** — own the narrative: press release, blog, briefings. Use for strategically
  significant deals where the story matters (new capability, market signal).
- **Earnings-timed** — fold into the next earnings cycle. Use when the deal is material but not
  urgent, and bundling with results is cleaner for IR.
- **Reactive-only** — no proactive push; prepared holding statements if asked. Use for small,
  low-interest deals — but still handle required filings and internal/customer comms.

## Step 3 — Timing inputs
- **Material public-co deal** → 8-K clock starts at signing (Item 1.01). Coordinate announcement to
  the filing, not after.
- **Market hours** → typically announce pre-market or post-close of trading for material deals (IR call).
- **Sign-to-close gap** → if deferred, decide whether to announce at signing (usual for material deals)
  or hold to close (rare). Longer gap → announce at signing to control leak risk.
- **Target readiness** → seller employees must be notified before/at public announcement, never after.

## Step 4 — Sensitivities
- HSR-reportable → no posture implying integration before close (gun-jumping).
- Competitive leak risk → faster, more controlled announcement; activate leak playbook early.
- Customer concentration → sequence key-customer outreach ahead of broad public comms.

Output: a one-paragraph posture decision (proactive / earnings / reactive) + timing anchor, carried
into the asset checklist and run-of-show.

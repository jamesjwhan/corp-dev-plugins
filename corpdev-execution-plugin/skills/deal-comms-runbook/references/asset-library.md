# Reference — Comms Asset Library

Select the assets the posture warrants; assign a DRI to each. Not every deal needs every asset — a
reactive-only acqui-hire may need only internal + seller-employee + a holding statement.

| Asset | Audience | Purpose | Typical DRI | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Press release | Public / press | Official announcement of record | Comms | Signing (material) |
| Blog post | Public / customers / talent | Narrative, the "why" | Product Marketing | Signing |
| Internal all-hands + Slack message | Toast employees | Inform, align, energize | Comms + Deal Sponsor | Signing, after seller employees |
| Seller-employee comms | Acquired team | Welcome, what changes, FAQ | People Ops + Deal Sponsor | **Before/at** public announce |
| Customer comms | Target's customers | Reassure continuity, what changes | Product Marketing + GTM | At/just after announce |
| External FAQ | Public / customers / press | Pre-empt questions | Comms | Signing |
| Internal FAQ | Toast employees / managers | Equip managers to answer | Comms + People Ops | Signing |
| Website banner / landing | Public | Visibility, SEO of the narrative | Web/Marketing | Signing or close |
| Social posts | Public | Amplify | Social/Comms | Signing |
| AMA / fireside | Toast + acquired team | Two-way, culture | Deal Sponsor | Signing week |
| IR / analyst comms | Investors, analysts | Financial framing, guidance impact | IR | Aligned to 8-K |
| Holding statement | Press (reactive) | Controlled response to inquiries/leaks | Comms | Standing, through the window |

## Sequencing principles
- **Seller employees are notified before the public.** Never let the acquired team learn from the news.
- **Tent → broader internal → employees → customers → public**, compressed into the run-of-show.
- Keep messaging consistent across assets (one source of truth for key messages); the FAQ anchors it.
- For each asset, the tracker holds: owner, draft status, **approval status** (nothing ships unapproved).

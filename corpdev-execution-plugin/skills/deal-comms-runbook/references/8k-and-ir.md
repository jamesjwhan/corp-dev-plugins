# Reference — 8-K & IR Sequencing

Toast is public (TOST), so material acquisitions carry SEC disclosure obligations that private
acquirers don't. **Materiality and filing decisions belong to Legal/IR — this is triage to route to
them, not a determination.**

## The two filings

| | Item 1.01 — Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement | Item 2.01 — Completion of Acquisition |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | **Signing** a material definitive agreement | **Completion** (closing) of a material acquisition |
| Deadline | Within **4 business days** of signing | Within **4 business days** of closing |
| Maps to | Stage 5 (announcement) | Stage 6 (close-day) |
| Note | The signing event for a material deal | Only if still material at close; may also pull in financial statements (Item 9.01) for larger deals |

## Materiality
Not a bright-line dollar test — it's a judgment on significance to investors (size relative to Toast,
strategic importance, financial impact). **Route to Legal/IR.** Most small tuck-ins/acqui-hires are
**not** material and need no 8-K; larger strategic deals will.

## Sequencing
1. Materiality assessment (Legal/IR) → determines whether 1.01 fires at signing.
2. If material: announcement and 8-K are coordinated — announce at/around the filing, pre-market or
   post-trading per IR.
3. IR/analyst comms aligned to the filing; prepare guidance-impact framing if the deal moves numbers.
4. At close: 2.01 if still material (and 9.01 financial statements for larger deals — confirm with IR).

## Reg FD / gun-jumping
- **Reg FD:** no selective disclosure of material non-public info ahead of the broad announcement.
- **Gun-jumping (if HSR-reportable):** no public posture implying integration or coordination before
  close; the clean team persists.

Output for the plan: the 8-K path (1.01 / 2.01 / none), the filing window, the IR sequence, and a
clear "route materiality to Legal/IR" flag.

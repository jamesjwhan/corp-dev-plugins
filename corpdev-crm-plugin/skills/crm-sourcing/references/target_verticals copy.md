# Target Verticals — CorpDev CRM

Shared reference file for `crm-market-pulse` and `crm-sourcing`. Update quarterly or when CRM P0/P1 priorities shift materially. Last updated: 2026-06-16.

---

## Primary Verticals

Scan deeply in both Market Pulse and company discovery.

| Vertical | Description | Key Sub-areas |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant POS | Core POS systems for restaurants, including international markets | SMB / indie, mid-market, enterprise chains, international (LATAM, APAC, Europe) |
| AI for Restaurants | AI-native applications across intelligence, voice, and vision | Voice AI (ordering, drive-thru, reservations), Vision AI (throughput, safety, ops), Intelligence (forecasting, menu optimization, labor analytics) |
| Enterprise POS | POS systems targeting enterprise chains (50+ locations) | QSR, fast casual, casual dining enterprise, franchise groups |
| Retail POS | POS systems for non-restaurant retail verticals | Convenience stores, beauty / spa, specialty retail |
| Consumer | Consumer-facing apps and platforms in the restaurant / food space | Consumer dining apps, food discovery, direct-to-consumer restaurant products |
| Tables / Reservations | Table management, reservations, and waitlist platforms | Online reservations, table management, waitlist, dine-in experience |

---

## Secondary Verticals

Adjacent to core focus. Scan for signals but apply lighter effort than primary verticals.

| Vertical | Description | Key Sub-areas |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant BOH | Back-of-house operations software | Accounting, inventory management, workforce management (scheduling, payroll, hiring / onboarding) |
| Restaurant Guest FOH | Front-of-house guest-facing technology | Digital ordering, kiosk, loyalty programs, CRM |
| SMB Fintech | Financial services targeting SMBs in food / hospitality | Payments, banking, lending, working capital |
| Restaurant Suppliers | Technology for food and beverage suppliers and distributors | Supply chain, procurement, distribution platforms |
